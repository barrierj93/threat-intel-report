This interactive redesign was created from a dashboard screenshot, not its underlying dataset. The bundled records are synthetic and must not be cited as evidence of real attacks.

01 / THE REFERENCE

A 2012–2023 attack series

The original screenshot establishes the visual subject, time window and analytical categories. Its visible annual counts total 152, with a 2012 peak of 57. The demo reuses these values as an illustrative incident distribution; this does not reconstruct the underlying study. The screenshot does not display 2018; its value is set to zero only for this prototype. The country assignments, targets, dates, severity and relationships are illustrative. The 127 unique target names are invented placeholders.

02 / CONNECTED ANALYSIS

One selection. Every perspective.

Search, country, attack type, institution sector and period filters combine. KPIs use filtered incident records; institutions are deduplicated by name. The sector chart counts incidents by sector, not unique institutions. The timeline retains all years as context while highlighting your period. Annual mode shows individual year counts; Cumulative mode sums the archive up to each year, before the period filter. Drag across the annual timeline to isolate a time window. Attack-method and sector comparisons ignore their own filter to keep alternatives selectable. Country rankings retain other filters but ignore the country selection, so comparisons remain possible. Saved-only applies to the register and its CSV export. In Compare, CSV exports both comparison countries under the active period, method, sector and search filters.

03 / YOUR DATA

Replace the demo archive

Import a JSON array or a comma- or semicolon-separated CSV with these fields. Use the two-letter country codes covered by the embedded map or country selector. Sector must be Public or Private; severity must be Low, Medium, High or Critical. Date must be a valid YYYY-MM-DD. Every ID must be unique. Optional: campaign.

id,date,country,institution,type,sector,severity,campaign DEMO-001,2023-05-17,US,Example institution,DDoS,Public,High,Example campaign

Import and export happen locally in your browser. Imported data stays in this session; download your source to keep it. Saved record IDs are stored separately for each archive in this browser when available.

04 / CARTOGRAPHY & ACCESSIBILITY

Country boundaries use Natural Earth 1:110m data (public domain). The globe uses an orthographic projection and the flat atlas uses Robinson. Map points identify representative country locations rather than exact attack origins or targets. The country/year matrix retains all years and countries as context while method, sector and search filters apply. Comparison uses its own two country selectors. Keyboard users can select countries through the filter, and reduced-motion preferences are respected.
